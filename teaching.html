Teaching

My teaching is guided by the Chinese educational principle jiaoxue xiangzhang: teaching and learning grow together. I create structured opportunities for students to turn curiosity into disciplined inquiry, then use their questions, work, and feedback to refine my teaching. Across substantive and methods courses, I help students formulate meaningful questions, evaluate competing explanations, and draw defensible conclusions from evidence. Concrete cases, transparent expectations, structured discussion, and scaffolded assignments allow me to pair high expectations with clear support.
