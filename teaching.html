Teaching

My teaching is guided by the Chinese educational principle jiaoxue xiangzhang (教学相长): teaching and learning grow together. I design courses that help students turn curiosity into disciplined inquiry by formulating meaningful questions, evaluating competing explanations, and drawing defensible conclusions from evidence. Concrete cases, transparent expectations, structured discussion, and scaffolded assignments provide clear pathways for meeting high expectations.
